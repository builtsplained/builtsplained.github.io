Satu Pintu Studio

One door for all our channels.

Satu Pintu Studio is a small independent studio that makes original short videos. Every channel is produced, checked and published through one shared pipeline ("satu pintu" means "one door").

Builtsplained: how things are built, explained. 3D animated explainers of machines and everyday technology (English).
  • YouTube: Builtsplained
  • TikTok: @builtsplained
  • Instagram: @builtsplained
Gimana Kalau Oki: Indonesian story-driven "what if" shorts.
  • YouTube and Instagram: @gimanakalauoki

Satu Pintu Publisher

Satu Pintu Publisher is our internal tool. It runs on our own computer and uploads videos we made ourselves to our own accounts:

  • our YouTube channels, through the YouTube Data API;
  • our TikTok accounts (such as @builtsplained), through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API;
  • our Instagram accounts, through the Instagram Graph API.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users. It does not read, collect or post anything on behalf of anyone else.
