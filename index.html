Builtsplained Publisher

Builtsplained Publisher is an internal tool that runs on our own computer. It uploads videos we made ourselves to our own accounts:

  • our YouTube channels (Builtsplained, Gimana Kalau Oki), through the YouTube Data API;
  • our TikTok account @builtsplained, through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users. It does not read, collect or post anything on behalf of anyone else.
