Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy covers Satu Pintu Publisher (Google Cloud project "channel-oki"), the internal tool described on our home page. It runs only on our own computer, and its only user is Satu Pintu Studio, the owner of the channels listed on our home page.

Google / YouTube

  • We use the scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and youtube.force-ssl only with the owner's consent through Google OAuth.
  • We use them to upload our own videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, post a comment on our own videos, and confirm which of our channels is signed in.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on our computer, in files readable only by the owner. They are sent to no one but Google.
  • Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We also follow the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok

  • We use TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish, only for our own TikTok accounts (such as @builtsplained).
  • Data we receive: the account's open_id, display name and avatar, and an access token and refresh token.
  • Tokens are stored only on our computer, in files readable only by the owner. We use them only to check that the right account is signed in and to publish our own videos to it.
  • Revoke access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

Instagram (Meta)

  • We use the Instagram Graph API through a Meta Business system user, only for our own Instagram accounts, to publish our own Reels.
  • The access token is stored only on our computer, in a file readable only by the owner.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data from viewers, other users or other accounts.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while publishing is active. They are deleted from our computer when access is revoked or the tool is retired. Questions or deletion requests: gimanakalauoki@gmail.com.
